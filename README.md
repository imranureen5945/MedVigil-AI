# MedVigil-AI
It's an AI-powered medicine safety assistant designed to help Pakistani households make safer and more informed decisions about medication use. It analyzes medicines in the context of a user’s existing medications, allergies, health conditions, and medication history to identify potential drug interactions, duplicate ingredients, dosage-related concerns, and condition-specific risks. The platform supports natural-language input in English and Roman Urdu, making it more accessible to everyday users. It also provides DRAP recall and registered-medicine information, medication safety guidance, self-medication risk assessment, and longitudinal safety insights. When a situation requires professional judgment, MedVigil AI connects users with healthcare professionals rather than attempting to prescribe or replace a doctor. Overall, the platform aims to reduce preventable medication-related harm and promote safer medication practices at the household level.
